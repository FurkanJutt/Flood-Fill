The main click contains approximately 270ms of leading silence; Back and Confirm
contain approximately 100ms. Their full files last 0.8–1.8 seconds. Previously,
rapid taps could start multiple long cues whose delayed onsets were heard after
the user stopped tapping. Per-kind concurrency/interval limits did not prevent
stacking across different UI sound kinds. The source pool also reclaimed sources
that had not yet reported their first playing DSP block.

Fix:
- Prepare cached PCM clips for UI feedback at startup/explicit audio Refresh.
  Extract the first audible burst, retain a 5ms pre-roll and a 15ms tail, apply
  2ms edge fades, and cap the result at the configurable duration (default 250ms).
  Original audio assets and SoundSettings clip references remain unchanged.
- Preparation reads at most the first two seconds of decompressed source audio;
  streaming/unreadable clips retain the original fallback. Use decompressed clips
  for immediate UI feedback. No PCM reads, clip creation or waiting happen per tap.
- All interaction feedback shares an 80ms default cooldown and one-frame duplicate
  guard. Excess taps are dropped. Accepted new taps replace the active feedback
  voice. There is no deferred retry or sound queue.
- Explicitly stop pending UI feedback when the app loses focus/suspends so it does
  not resume later. Reserve newly started pooled sources until their first DSP
  playback is observed; cancel unsuccessful pending startup after a short grace.
- Music and gameplay/result/flood cues retain their existing clips/lifetimes and
  separate concurrency policy. Master/SFX mute and volume routing remain in use.
- Configure UI Feedback Minimum Interval / Maximum Duration under UI Sound Effects
  on FloodAudioManager. Reapply Audio Settings rebuilds prepared clips after edits.
